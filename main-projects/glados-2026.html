Overview

GLADOS was designed and built for consistent match performance through the full 2026 season. The robot combined a 365-degree continuous turret, a 100+ fuel hopper, and fast 10+ fuel/second shooting to reduce cycle time and keep scoring pressure high without constant chassis re-aiming. Built around reliability and serviceability, it earned the Quality Award at Granite State, the Creativity Award at NEDCMP, and finished as a Burns Division Finalist.
